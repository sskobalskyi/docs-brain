# Acme customer refund and shipping policy

## Refunds
Refunds are issued within 14 days of purchase when the product is unused and in its original packaging. After approval the money is returned to the original payment method within five business days. Digital products are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.

## Exchanges
Items can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery. The first exchange is free, later exchanges cost a flat fee of 5 euros. Exchanges depend on stock availability.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the European Union. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 euros. Orders above 80 euros ship for free with standard delivery. International orders outside the EU take 7 to 14 business days and may be subject to customs fees.

## Warranty
All hardware products include a two year warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Damage caused by accidents, liquids or unauthorised repairs is not covered. To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and photos of the defect.

## Contact
Support is available on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 by email and chat. Urgent delivery problems are answered within four hours.
