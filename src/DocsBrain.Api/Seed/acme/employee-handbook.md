# Acme employee handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside the core hours everyone chooses their own schedule as long as the team agrees on availability. Overtime must be approved by a manager in advance and is compensated with time off.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The remaining two days are spent in the office so that teams can meet in person. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head and are reviewed every six months.

## Vacation
Every full-time employee receives 25 paid vacation days per calendar year. Unused days, up to five, can be carried over to the next year until the end of March. Vacation requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks before the planned start date.

## Sick leave
Employees receive ten paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive days of absence. Please notify your manager before the start of the working day.

## Expenses
Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days after the receipt is submitted in the expense tool. Flights and hotels must be booked through the travel desk. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 50 euros per day.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel counts as business travel and follows the expense rules above.
