# Globex onboarding guide

## First day
New employees receive their laptop and accounts on the first day. The IT desk is open from 09:00 and walks everyone through the password manager and multi-factor setup. Lunch with the team is on the company.

## Mentor
Every newcomer gets a mentor from their own team for the first three months. Mentors meet their mentee once a week for 30 minutes and help with questions about tools, processes and culture.

## The 30-60-90 day plan
In the first 30 days you learn the product and ship a small change. By day 60 you own a small area and join the on-call shadow rotation. By day 90 you set goals for the next half year together with your manager.

## Benefits
Globex offers private health insurance from the first day, a sports allowance of 40 euros per month and 28 vacation days per year. The company covers a home office setup of up to 600 euros for remote employees.

## Probation
The probation period lasts three months. A feedback meeting takes place after the first month and a final review in the last week of probation.
